Six games for the price of none

The idea was pieced together after I'd come across a Reddit post about there not being a 'Final Destination' game, so I decided to make one! I knew I couldn't create and manipulate 3D environments yet, but I did know I could write my ass off, so I decided on a branching text adventure. A single shell script about a deadly house ("House Trap") was the starting point.
As functions piled up, the need to refactor turned one script into a main.py entry point with a dedicated hazard engine to handle environmental dangers, the need for more reactivity led to the integration of a QTE engine, and so on until before I knew it, the project had grown into a full-fledged game-agnostic engine with bootstrapping, assets, animation, JSON game data for SIX games I am also developing, data verification on init, debugging logs and more!

The most complete game so far. Do you believe a stranger's vision? A bridge collapsing, an escalator turning into a meat grinder, whatever catastrophe the shuffled deck deals that round.
Disrupt the design by helping people escape, letting the chaos unfold, or walking away entirely. Now Death is correcting it, one name at a time, and you're the only one who ever saw it coming.

Among Us with a twist. An Antarctic research outpost, cut off for the season, twelve people who all know each other ..or so they think. Something arrived with a helicopter crash, and it can wear a face perfectly enough that even the people who see it every day can't tell. Investigate, run tests, confront people you have to keep working alongside no matter what you suspect. If you keep getting it wrong, you'll tank your reputation and risk handing the thing your whole crew.

A unique take on the choose-your-own-adventure genre. A true branching-node adventure, not a linear game with the occasional fork — every scene is a real decision point, and every decision can save someone or cost them. Death's shuffled a kill order for your friend group behind the scenes, and the only warning is a phone call you can choose to ignore.

Warning! Rated 17+. Contains graphic text and 'South Park'-style animated depictions of death, injury, and gore; psychological horror; mature language; dark themes. Not suitable for children.
